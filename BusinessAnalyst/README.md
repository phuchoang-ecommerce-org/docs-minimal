# Business Analyst

Read [product scope](./product-scope.md), [business rules](./business-rules.md), [use cases](./use-cases.md), [activity diagrams](./activity-diagrams.md), [BPMN 2.0 process](./bpmn-process.md), then [user stories](./user-stories.md). IDs in this folder are the business source for the [OpenAPI contract](../SolutionArchitecture/api-contract/index.md) and [test scenarios](../TestingBenchmark/testing-scenarios.md).

The only human roles are Guest, Member, Seller and Marketplace Admin. All prices are USD. Checkout is Member-only and COD-only. Shipping, returns and refunds are outside the application. Product images have LINK and UPLOAD modes.
